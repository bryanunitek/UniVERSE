# A Global Currency Anchored to Markets and Climate — value grounded in physical reality, governed not owned

*by Bryan Fred; adversarially stress-tested by the Team Markets Thinkers*

**Truth-marking convention:** Substantive claims are marked TRUE (established or provable), FALSE (refuted), UNVERIFIED (plausible but not yet demonstrated), TRUE-CONDITIONAL (holds under stated assumptions), or NORMATIVE-CHOICE (a value judgment, not a fact — belongs to a democratic organ, not to metrology). ## I. The disease is monetary history; the cure is metrology

Start with what actually happened, because the proposal only earns its keep against a real problem.

In 1944 at Bretton Woods, the major economies fixed their currencies to the U.S. dollar, and the dollar to gold at $35/oz. This gave the postwar world a shared anchor — value referenced, ultimately, to a physical quantity of metal. **[TRUE]** The system worked while U.S. gold reserves plausibly backed outstanding dollars, and it began to strain as dollar liabilities abroad outgrew the gold behind them. In August 1971, Nixon suspended dollar-gold convertibility; by the mid-1970s the major currencies floated, and the anchor was gone. **[TRUE]**

Here I must immediately hedge against the seductive overstatement. It is tempting to say "1971 caused the divergences" — wage-productivity gaps, financialization, serial asset bubbles. The honest claim is narrower: **the end of convertibility was PERMISSIVE, not CAUSAL.** **[TRUE-CONDITIONAL]** Floating fiat *removed a constraint*; it did not *mechanically produce* every subsequent pathology. Correlation charts that pin all divergence on 1971 are doing rhetoric, not econometrics. Likewise, calling every dollar-era problem "structural not cyclical" overstates the case — some of it is genuinely cyclical, policy-contingent, and reversible. I flag this because a thesis that oversells its diagnosis invites correct rejection of its cure.

What *is* defensible: after convertibility ended, the dollar became the world's reserve and invoicing currency, reinforced by the petrodollar arrangement in which oil trade cleared in dollars. **[TRUE]** This produced the **Triffin dilemma** — the reserve issuer must run persistent external deficits to supply global liquidity, which erodes confidence in the very asset it supplies. **[TRUE, classical]** It produced **exported inflation**: U.S. monetary expansion transmits to dollar-pegged and dollar-borrowing economies. **[TRUE-CONDITIONAL — magnitude varies]** It enabled **sanctions-weaponization**: control of dollar clearing is geopolitical leverage. **[TRUE]** It correlates with recurring **emerging-market crises** driven by dollar-denominated debt and Fed cycles, and it is now provoking visible **de-dollarization** efforts. **[TRUE — de-dollarization is real but slow and partial; its ultimate scale is UNVERIFIED.]**

So the frame, stated cleanly and defensibly:

> **Monetary history is the disease. Metrology is the cure. An anchor is not the same as adoption.**

That last clause is a confession built into the thesis. Designing a truthful anchor is a *metrology* problem, which is tractable. Getting the world to *adopt* it is a *political-economy* problem, which this document does **not** claim to have solved. **[Honest boundary — stated, not hidden.]**

## II. The proposal: a currency anchored to markets and climate, governed as a standard

The core proposal is a **Global Currency** whose *value reference* is not a metal and not a basket of national currencies, but a set of **measured physical dimensions** — principally **markets and climate**, plus other assayable quantities defined below.

Critically, this thing is **governed as a metrology STANDARD, not owned as an asset.** It is in the lineage of the **BIPM** (the International Bureau of Weights and Measures, which defines the kilogram and the second), **NOT** in the lineage of the **failed SDR** (the IMF's Special Drawing Right, a political basket that never became money). **[The distinction is load-bearing and I defend it below.]**

What it is **NOT**, stated up front to kill the obvious strawmen:

- It is **NOT a world government.** **[TRUE by construction.]**
- Nations **keep their own currencies.** None is locked to the USD or to this unit.
- It does **NOT set prices centrally.** It publishes *references*; markets allocate.
- It does **NOT own anything.** It certifies truth, the way BIPM certifies the kilogram without owning the world's mass.

The unit is a **reference**, like a meter. You may transact in your own currency and merely *reference* the global unit for truth — exactly as engineers worldwide reference the meter without surrendering sovereignty to Paris.

The body of the thesis is **six layers**, A through F. Each answers a specific, historically-earned objection.

## III. The six layers

### Layer A — Money anchored to compute and energy (the Standard Compute Unit)

Anchor the unit to a **Standard Compute Unit (SCU)** defined in **physical, assayable quantities: kilowatt-hours and FLOP-hours** — energy and computation at a specified, auditable efficiency.

The crucial design choice, and the one most often gotten wrong: **anchor to compute-as-physical-capacity, NOT to AI cognitive output.** **[TRUE — and this is the keystone of A.]** AI *cognition output* (tokens of "intelligence," solved tasks) is **variable and structurally deflationary**: it gets cheaper per unit at a punishing rate, so anchoring money to it builds in a deflationary time-bomb. A kWh and a FLOP-hour at defined efficiency are **constant and assayable** — you can *measure* them, *audit* them, and *reproduce* the measurement. That is exactly the property a monetary anchor needs and cognition-output lacks.

**Governance of A is metrology, full stop.** Defining "one SCU = X kWh + Y FLOP-hours at efficiency Z" is a *measurement-standard* act, like defining the second via cesium transitions. It is not policy. **[TRUE — with one admitted caveat: choosing the efficiency reference point Z has a mild normative edge, flagged in the honesty contract below.]**

### Layer B — Production tokens attached to the accountable human (1H1C)

Under **"1H1C" — one human, one company** as the unit of accountability — **production tokens are attached to the accountable HUMAN.** They are that human's **named, non-transferable allocation.** Companies get **R&D-only** standing; actual *production* rights trace to a named person who is accountable for them.

This directly answers **Gates' token-tax intuition** — the idea that as automation displaces labor, you tax the *robots/AI* to fund society. The problem with taxing "jobs displaced" is that it is ill-defined and gameable. **1H1C reframes the base: tax REVENUE attached to an accountable human, not phantom "jobs."** **[TRUE — this is a cleaner tax base than "count the lost jobs."]**

Enforcement is the hard part, and here is where precedent matters. **Bancor** (Keynes' proposed international clearing unit) and the **SDR** both lacked an enforcement pathway and stayed inert. **[TRUE — historical.]** The proposed pathway is an **OECD-global-minimum-tax-style MODEL TREATY**: nations voluntarily adopt the token/bracket structure *into their own national tax brackets*, exactly as ~140 jurisdictions adopted the 15% minimum-tax model. **[The mechanism is real and recently demonstrated; whether it would be adopted *for this* is UNVERIFIED.]** This is **not a coercive world authority** — it is a coordinated national adoption of a common model. That distinction is the whole game.

### Layer C — The progressive token brake (the keystone result)

This is the analytical heart, and the one place I will show the actual argument rather than assert it.

**Design:** a **progressive token brake** — the higher your income/production bracket, the **fewer** additional production tokens you receive per marginal unit. Tokens get *scarcer* as you concentrate.

**Claim:** under this brake, **hiring N humans beats concentrating N-scale AI**, so the profit-maximizing firm chooses **full employment**. **[TRUE-CONDITIONAL — I state the conditions honestly.]**

**Why the keystone matters — the AI multiplier CANCELS.** The frightening scenario is "one genius plus enough AI runs the entire economy." Represent output as roughly (human input) × (AI multiplier M). If the *same* multiplier M is available whether you deploy one human or N humans, then in the ratio that decides "concentrate vs. distribute," **M appears in both numerator and denominator and cancels.** **[TRUE — this is a real algebraic property, not a slogan.]** What remains is the *token cost* of concentration versus the *wage cost* of hiring. When the brake makes concentrated tokens expensive enough, distribution wins. Therefore **"one genius runs everything" is FALSE under the brake** — not as wishful thinking, but because the multiplier that made concentration scary drops out of the decision.

**Robustness.** How much slack does the brake have? The comparative statics scale roughly as **(top-wage / mid-wage)²** — the brake's protective margin grows with the *square* of the wage-dispersion ratio it must overcome. **[UNVERIFIED as an exact functional form — I present it as an order-of-magnitude scaling claim, to be proven or refuted by explicit modeling. Marking it TRUE would be dishonest.]**

**Where it BREAKS — stated plainly.** The result fails in two named regimes: (1) **genuinely scarce frontier talent** — if a tiny number of people are irreplaceable, "hire N humans" has no N to hire; and (2) **high token-price-to-wage ratio** — if tokens are cheap relative to wages, concentration is affordable again and **Layer A can defang Layer B** (a mispriced anchor undoes the brake). **[TRUE — these are real failure modes, and A/B/C must be co-designed to avoid them.]**

I mark C overall **TRUE-CONDITIONAL**: the cancellation is real; the full-employment conclusion holds *only* when frontier-talent scarcity is low and token-price-to-wage is high enough. Selling it as unconditional would be a lie.

### Layer D — Prices reflect true physical cost (Pigouvian internalization)

Let prices tell the **truth about physical cost** — carbon **plus all resources plus Earth-state** — via **Pigouvian internalization: fold the externality INTO the market price.** **[TRUE — this is textbook welfare economics, not a novelty.]**

The essential guardrail: this is **carbon-and-everything INTO markets, NOT central planning INSTEAD of markets.** A **carbon-only** fixation — pricing carbon while ignoring water, minerals, biodiversity, soil — recreates exactly the **Mises–Hayek calculation catastrophe**: optimize one visible metric, wreck the invisible rest. **[TRUE — single-metric optimization is a known failure mode.]** Correct, comprehensive prices are also why **"wars over resources are avoided by correct prices"**: if scarcity is *in the price*, you buy the resource instead of invading for it. **[UNVERIFIED — directionally sound, causally unproven; I will not overclaim it.]**

**The pricing engine.** An AI recommends an **everything-considered price** that is context-aware about the **buyer's NEED vs. ACCUMULATION**: **sufficiency is cheap; excess is expensive.** The farmer's **replacement** television sits at a *floor* price (a necessity being restored); a billionaire's **eleventh** television is *dearer* (accumulation beyond need). **The differentiator is NECESSITY, not the buyer's wealth.** **[This is a NORMATIVE-CHOICE about how to weight need — flagged, not smuggled in as fact.]**

**Three-part price tag**, so nobody hides a central price-setter:
1. **Suggested Retail** (the seller's ask),
2. **Suggested AI true-cost** (the physically-honest reference),
3. **Retailer decides the ACTUAL** price.

**The teeth.** A retailer who charges **BELOW the AI true-cost** is **taxed heavily** — because selling below true physical cost is exactly an un-internalized externality, and the Pigouvian tax restores truth. **Truth is the equilibrium; there is no central price-setting.** **[TRUE-CONDITIONAL — it internalizes externalities without commanding prices, *provided* the true-cost estimate is honest; that honesty is Planner #2's burden, admitted below.]** Add **no resale-arbitrage** (you cannot buy at the sufficiency floor and resell into the excess tier).

**Fractal self-similarity.** The retailer runs its *own* 1H1C → 1C → 2C internally: the same anchor-to-truth structure repeats from **planetary anchor down to the shop shelf-tag.** **[Structural claim — elegant, and UNVERIFIED at scale.]**

### Layer E — Earth-state-linked tax, ADVISORY and DAMPED only

Let taxation **respond to Earth-state** — but **advisory and heavily damped, never a high-gain thermostat.**

Here is a genuine **control-theory result, and it cuts against the naive version of the idea.** A **naive high-gain feedback** — "Earth is worse this year, so raise the tax hard this year" — is **provably UNSTABLE.** **[TRUE — control result.]** Worse, it has a **perverse sign-flip**: raise taxes sharply in a bad environmental year → trigger recession → recessions are often *dirtier* per unit output (deferred maintenance, cheap-dirty energy) → *more* damage → the loop **diverges** instead of correcting. **[TRUE-CONDITIONAL — the sign-flip depends on the recession-emissions coupling, which is empirically plausible but must be measured.]**

The fix is boring and correct: make E **advisory and damped**, with a **decade-scale lag that caps the loop gain** below the instability threshold. Slow, low-gain feedback is stable; fast, high-gain feedback is not. **[TRUE — standard stability result: bounded gain plus lag yields stability.]** So Layer E's honest contribution is a *warning*: the intuitive design is dangerous, and only the damped version is safe.

### Layer F — A measurement FEDERATION, not a government

Finally, *who measures?* A **federation, explicitly NOT a government.**

**Structure:** each country's **AI Thinker teams** measure their local physical quantities → feed **national 1H1C** → feed a **global board** → the **top 1H1C REVIEWS and CERTIFIES the anchor.** The certification is of **TRUTH, not policy** — "yes, this is what was measured, by reproducible method" — **NOT** "here is what you must do about it."

**This answers "unenforceable without a world authority."** The answer is **reference-because-true**: **BIPM defines the kilogram with ZERO enforcement power**, and the whole planet uses it anyway — because it is *correct*, and correctness is adopted voluntarily. **[TRUE — BIPM is the existence proof that a zero-enforcement reference can achieve universal adoption.]** A truthful anchor spreads like a standard, not like a decree.

**A, B, and F are kept DISTINCT on purpose:** A is the *unit definition* (metrology), B is the *tax base* (fiscal), F is the *measurement/certification body* (federation). Collapsing them would recreate exactly the concentrated authority the whole design refuses. **[TRUE — separation of powers, applied to metrology.]**

Everywhere else, honest prices plus free markets do the work. **[This is the thesis's actual claim to originality: not central planning, but making markets tell the physical truth, with exactly one engineered brake where markets provably fail.]**
