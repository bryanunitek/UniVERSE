## Scope

This file sets out the time horizon of the UniVERSE programme as a whole — civilisational-scale adoption, governmental and multilateral engagement, translation into working policy. The canonical horizon statement is published in the TrueAI repository at [`HORIZON.md`](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI/blob/main/HORIZON.md). This file extends that statement to the programme level.

## What is ready today

- The **UniVERSE** programme materials — whitepapers, founding statement, governance model, succession charter — are published and citable.
- The **TrueAI Foundation** invariants are stable, published, and usable now as a reference standard.
- The **UniCORE AI** reference architecture — the 12-level governance stack — is published in full.

These are the written-down artefacts. They are open for challenge, translation, adaptation, and implementation under CC BY 4.0.

## What is not ready today

- No known AI system currently satisfies all of the TrueAI Foundation invariants at production conformance.
- No jurisdiction has yet adopted the programme into statute, regulation, or formal procurement policy.
- No independent certification body yet exists to audit conformance claims.

**This is expected.** Civilisational governance programmes are not proven at launch. They are proven by prolonged use under real-world stress across many institutions.

## Time horizon

The programme's time-to-maturity is measured in decades, not quarters. A realistic horizon for programme-level adoption:

- First partial implementations reaching production use in narrow domains: **1–3 years**.
- First independent audit frameworks, academic citation in policy work, and early governmental pilots: **3–7 years**.
- First jurisdictional uptake into formal policy or procurement requirements: **5–10 years**.
- The programme reaching the maturity that would let it function as a genuine civilisational governance layer, across multiple sectors and jurisdictions: **10–20 years, possibly longer**.

This is not a delay. It is the nature of the work.

For comparison: Underwriters Laboratories has been certifying electrical safety for over 130 years. The Kitemark has been operating for over 120 years. Fairtrade for over 35. Civilisational governance frameworks become trustworthy by testing across many systems, many jurisdictions, many adversaries, and many edge cases, over many years. There is no path that shortcuts that.

## Why publish now

Two reasons.

First, the written foundation is stable and ready to be cited, challenged, extended, and adopted as a reference. The long clock does not start running until that foundation exists. Publication is how that clock starts.

Second, this is the author's considered view of where humanity will need to be in 10–20 years in its relationship with artificial intelligence. The programme exists because that future is coming whether it is prepared for or not. Publishing the foundation now is an act of preparation, not an act of shipping a product.
