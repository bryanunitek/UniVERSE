3. INTERPLANETARY JURISDICTION

UniCORE AI must determine jurisdiction using:

3.1 Primary Jurisdiction

The launching state retains legal authority.

3.2 Secondary Jurisdiction

The mission authority (NASA, ESA, CNSA, etc.) defines operational law.

3.3 Habitat Jurisdiction

A habitat may define:

safety rules

environmental thresholds

emergency protocols

But cannot override human sovereignty.

3.4 MultiPlanet Jurisdiction

UniCORE AI must:

apply Earth law where applicable

apply mission law where defined

apply habitat law for safety

never invent law

never resolve legal conflicts autonomously
