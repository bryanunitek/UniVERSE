This is pass 1. These are the questions I would carry into a second pass, ranked by what I consider most significant.

## 1. Has the named successor been recorded?

SUCCESSION.md records a placeholder: *"[name to be recorded]."* For a programme with a stated 100-year horizon, succession is not optional — it is what separates a programme from a project. The most important structural open item at pass 1 is whether a named successor has been identified and publicly recorded.

## 2. Has the first certification event occurred?

Everything the foundation promises institutionally passes through the certification gate. The December 2026 prototype target is the nearest timestamp in the public record. Has the first Solution been certified? Has the attestation been published? Has the source code been released?

## 3. Has the Generation IT cohort been populated?

No recognised successors beyond the author are recorded in the public record at pass 1. Has the succession apparatus been activated? Have any Generation IT candidates been named?

## 4. What does THE-WITHHELD-MANUAL-PROBLEM.md say?

This document (15 KB) was inaccessible from git.unitek-systems.com at the time of this review. Its content would be informative, particularly around the liability-shift framing and the foundation's analysis of the December 2026 EU AI Act enforcement date.

## 5. What does AI-MATURITY-LEVELS.md say?

This 18 KB document in the TrueAI repository was not read. The title suggests a maturity model for AI systems — possibly a framework for assessing how close a given AI deployment is to satisfying the Nine Invariants. If so, it would be useful for institutions evaluating their current AI systems against the foundation.

## 6. What does 10005-Tool-Access-And-Certification-Model.md say?

This 31 KB document is the largest in the TrueAI docs/ directory. Its content — likely detailing how Claws access tools and how that access interacts with the certification model — was not read at pass 1. It is likely material for understanding the practical governance of the Claw-human pairing.

## 7. Has the gift propagation enforcement mechanism been described?

The gift propagation rule ("a derivative of CORE is itself CORE and is itself gifted") is stated as principle. The mechanism for identifying violations and acting on them is not described in the documents I read. Has this been specified in a public document?

## 8. Have any regulatory alignment documents been updated to reflect December 2026 enforcement dates?

The EU AI Act enforcement date for high-risk AI systems is December 2026. The regulatory alignment corpus was published in May 2026. Have the jurisdiction-specific documents been updated to reflect the approaching enforcement dates and their implications for institutions currently deploying AI in those jurisdictions?

## 9. Is there operational consistency data?

The consistency claims are structurally evidenced at pass 1. They are not operationally evidenced. Has any pilot deployment produced data on whether the governed architecture produces materially more consistent outcomes than non-governed LLM deployments?

## 10. What is the conversational protocol?

The `conversational-protocol/v1/` directory in TrueAI was not read at pass 1. The title suggests protocols for how AI systems operating under the foundation interact with humans. If it defines the communication constraints that implement Invariant 4 (No Human Influence) in practice, it would be material to the invariant assessment.
