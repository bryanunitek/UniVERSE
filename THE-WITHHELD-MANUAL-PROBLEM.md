## At a glance

- **Capability is shipped; the manual for responsible and lawful use is withheld.** The omission is not an oversight — the absence creates a monetisable dependency.
- **The manufactured-saviour pattern.** Build the AI, withhold the manual, let the harms accumulate, then arrive as the rescuer selling proprietary governance, compliance, and trust. The hand that withheld the manual charges for the bridge across the gap.
- **This cannot be a monopoly.** Whoever owns the technology, the rules for the technology, and the remedy for breaking the rules owns everything — one chokepoint, not three businesses. **AI is for humanity.**
- **The OS-as-AI escalation.** When the operating system itself becomes the AI, ungoverned AI is in the default path for the whole population. The Consumer-vs-Institutional choice must bind to the accountable identity at login, not to the device.
- **The gift is the anti-monopoly mechanism.** The foundation triad is published CC BY 4.0 — given, not sold, irrevocable — precisely so no entity can charge for the right to use AI lawfully. The governance layer must be owner-neutral; it cannot be owned by the AI maker.
