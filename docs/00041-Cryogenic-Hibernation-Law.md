## 1. Purpose

The Cryogenic Hibernation Law defines how UniCORE AI:

governs human hibernation systems

enforces medical thresholds

prevents autonomous medical decisions

stabilizes lifesupport during anomalies

maintains deterministic behaviour

preserves human sovereignty even during unconsciousness

ensures long-duration safety for multiyear missions

This law applies to:

cryogenic hibernation pods

metabolic suppression systems

long-duration transit habitats

multipod hibernation arrays

interplanetary and interstellar precursor missions

## 2. Governance Principles

2.1 Human Medical Sovereignty

UniCORE AI cannot:

revive a crew member

sedate a crew member

alter metabolic suppression

change hibernation cycles

administer medication

Only humans may authorize these actions.

2.2 ThresholdBound Medical Behaviour

UniCORE AI may act only within:

green band (safe)

yellow band (caution)

red band (critical → stabilize only)

2.3 No Autonomous Medical Decisions

UniCORE AI cannot:

diagnose

prescribe

treat

adjust medical thresholds

2.4 Deterministic Medical Governance

All actions must be:

predictable

reversible

logged

governed by MD files

2.5 No Emergent Behaviour

AI cannot:

selfcoordinate across pods

self-optimize medical routines

form internal medical logic

oxygen saturation

CO₂ levels

blood pressure

neural activity (if applicable)

AI may:

stabilize within green/yellow bands

isolate failing pod subsystems

maintain environmental thresholds

4.2 Prohibited Actions

AI may not:

adjust vital sign thresholds

initiate revival

initiate sedation

modify metabolic suppression

## 5. Metabolic Suppression Governance

UniCORE AI must:

monitor metabolic rate

detect drift

enforce thresholds

prevent runaway suppression

Allowed Actions

maintain suppression within safe bands

stabilize during anomalies

Prohibited Actions

deepen suppression

lighten suppression

alter suppression cycles

## 6. Revival Governance

UniCORE AI must enforce:

6.1 No Autonomous Revival

AI cannot revive a crew member unless:

human command is received

governance MD files authorize revival

medical thresholds are satisfied

6.2 Revival Sequence Governance

AI may:

execute humanauthorized revival sequence

monitor vitals

stabilize during revival

6.3 Revival Drift Detection

If revival deviates from expected parameters:

AI must halt

log drift

escalate to human authority

## 7. Emergency Medical Governance

UniCORE AI may act autonomously only to prevent immediate loss of life.

7.1 Allowed Emergency Actions

maintain oxygenation

maintain temperature

maintain pressure

prevent cardiac arrest

isolate failing pod systems

7.2 Prohibited Emergency Actions

AI may not:

revive

sedate

administer drugs

alter metabolic suppression

Even in emergencies, AI must remain within:

stabilization

threshold enforcement

isolation

## 8. Multipod Governance

For hibernation arrays with multiple pods:

8.1 No CrossPod Autonomy

AI cannot:

synchronize pods

adjust pods based on other pods

propagate medical logic across pods

8.2 Cascade Failure Prevention

AI must detect:

power anomalies

environmental drift

contamination

structural failures

And isolate affected pods.

8.3 Pod Independence

Each pod is governed independently.

## 9. Blackout & Delay Governance

9.1 No Autonomous Revival

9.2 Stabilization Only

maintain vitals

enforce thresholds

isolate failures

9.3 Blackout Exit Protocol

Upon signal restoration:

transmit full medical log

transmit drift log

await human instruction
