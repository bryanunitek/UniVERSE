# OUTREACH

Formal record of outreach made from the UniVERSE / TrueAI / UniCORE-AI
programme to external organisations.

UniVERSE, TrueAI, and UniCORE AI are **given, not sold, and irrevocable**.
This folder exists so that every formal offer of the work to an external
organisation is preserved in the same public, dated, versioned record that
holds the work itself.

## Policy

- No reply is required from any recipient. A non-reply is not a refusal.
  The offer stands, and the work remains available.
- Entries in this folder record **the fact of the offer**, not any private
  content a recipient may have sent back. Replies are referenced by
  status only, not quoted, unless the sender has given consent.
- Recipient email addresses recorded here are those already published by
  the recipient organisation as public contact points.
- Once written, entries in this folder are not edited except to correct
  factual errors or to update delivery status for a specific recipient as
  new information arrives.
