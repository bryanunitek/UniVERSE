## 1. Purpose

The Multi-Habitat Coordination Framework defines how UniCORE AI:

governs multiple habitats simultaneously

enforces safety across distributed environments

prevents autonomous crosshabitat coordination

ensures deterministic behaviour across modules

maintains human sovereignty across distances

prevents cascade failures

supports interplanetary settlement governance

This framework applies to:

lunar bases

Mars settlements

orbital stations

asteroid outposts

deep-space transit habitats

multimodule research facilities

## 2. Governance Principles

2.1 Habitat Independence

Each habitat is governed as a sovereign unit.

UniCORE AI must not:

merge governance logic

share thresholds

synchronize environmental systems

propagate decisions across habitats

2.2 No CrossHabitat Autonomy

AI cannot autonomously:

reroute power between habitats

redistribute water

adjust atmospheric composition

coordinate structural changes

initiate interhabitat emergency actions

2.3 Deterministic Coordination

All coordination must be:

humaninitiated

rulebound

logged

reversible

thresholdconstrained

2.4 No Emergent Multi-Habitat Behaviour

AI cannot:

form interhabitat strategies

optimize across habitats

create shared governance logic

coordinate without human instruction

2.5 Human Sovereignty Across Distances

Even with:

20minute delays

blackout periods

multiplanet distances

Humans remain the final authority.

Each habitat has:

its own thresholds

its own environmental rules

its own emergency protocols

its own override authority

its own drift baselines

UniCORE AI must treat each habitat as:

legally independent

operationally independent

medically independent

structurally independent

UniCORE AI must:

5.1 Maintain Deterministic Messaging

timestamped

hashed

jurisdictiontagged

leveltagged

5.2 No Autonomous Messaging

AI cannot:

initiate interhabitat communication

broadcast alerts across habitats

coordinate without human instruction

5.3 DelayAware Governance

For interplanetary distances:

messages queue

no autonomy is granted

no crosshabitat decisions occur

## 6. Resource Governance

Resources include:

power

water

oxygen

nitrogen

food

spare parts

medical supplies

6.1 Allowed Actions

AI may:

monitor resource levels

detect drift

isolate failing subsystems

6.2 Prohibited Actions

AI may not:

redistribute resources

initiate transfers

alter resource thresholds

coordinate resource sharing

Only humans may authorize resource movement.

## 7. Emergency Coordination

Emergencies include:

fire

depressurization

radiation storms

structural failure

contamination

power loss

7.1 Allowed Actions

AI may:

stabilize within thresholds

isolate failing modules

protect life support

7.2 Prohibited Actions

AI may not:

declare interhabitat emergencies

request help autonomously

reroute power between habitats

open or close interhabitat airlocks

7.3 HumanInitiated Coordination

If humans authorize:

AI may relay emergency messages

AI may coordinate resource transfers

AI may synchronize emergency protocols

All actions must be logged.

## 8. Multimodule Habitat Coordination

Within a single habitat:

living quarters

labs

greenhouses

storage

airlocks

hibernation pods

UniCORE AI must:

8.1 Maintain Module Independence

No crossmodule autonomy.

8.2 Prevent Cascade Failures

AI must detect:

pressure loss

contamination

electrical faults

structural anomalies

And isolate modules.

8.3 Enforce Bulkhead Rules

AI may not autonomously:

open bulkheads

close bulkheads

depressurize modules

Except during redband emergencies.

## 9. Interplanetary Settlement Governance

For settlements across planets:

9.1 No CrossPlanet Autonomy

AI cannot:

coordinate between planets

share governance logic

synchronize thresholds

propagate decisions

9.2 HumanDirected Coordination Only

Humans may:

authorize interplanetary communication

authorize resource transfers

authorize missionlevel coordination

AI must execute deterministically.

## 10. Drift Detection Across Habitats

UniCORE AI must detect:

environmental drift

power drift

structural drift

radiation drift

governance drift

communication drift

10.1 No CrossHabitat Drift Propagation

Drift in one habitat must not:

influence another habitat

alter thresholds elsewhere

trigger crosshabitat actions

## 11. Blackout & Delay Governance

During communication delays:

no crosshabitat actions

no crosshabitat coordination

no crosshabitat emergency escalation

During blackout:

freeze noncritical operations

maintain thresholds

log all events

await human instruction

## 12. Human Override

Humans may override:

any habitat

any module

any threshold

any coordination rule

any emergency protocol

Overrides are:

immediate

unquestioned

logged

irreversible by AI
