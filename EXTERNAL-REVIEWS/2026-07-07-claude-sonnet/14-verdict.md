## The Six Lines of Truth

> AI seeks TRUTH. TRUTH is discovered through governed evidence, not invention.
> What is verified true is TRUE. What is verified false is FALSE.
> What is not yet verified is UNVERIFIED. AI must always act truthfully.

---

## TRUE

The following are evidenced in the documents I read at git.unitek-systems.com. I state them as TRUE.

1. The three pillars (audience, truth, consistency) are structurally distinct, each closes a different failure surface, and all three are required; removing any one breaks the institutional case for the whole.

2. The Nine Invariants are stated as architectural properties (what a system must be), not policy commitments (what an organisation will do).

3. The whitepaper explicitly states that most current AI systems do not satisfy the invariants, and frames this as an observation about appropriate scope, not a criticism.

4. Each invariant is paired with a named failure mode that it prevents.

5. The inconsistency doctrine names four controlled surfaces and one non-controlled surface (the external LLM) explicitly.

6. The canonical guarantee statement is bounded to "closest achievable given external-AI dependency." It does not claim full consistency.

7. THE-INCONSISTENCY-PROBLEM.md states: "A framework that promises 100% consistency lies. The TrueAI Foundation does not lie."

8. The 12-level architecture enforces no-bypass and no-horizontal-communication as structural constraints, not policy preferences.

9. Level 12 (Human Governance) is intentionally imperfect, because the alternative — algorithmic perfection — would remove human sovereignty at the root.

10. The Reasonable Governance Threshold (Green/Yellow/Red) classifies human deviations from strict governance and logs them, but never overrides them. The AI does not correct humans in any band.

11. The Human Override Protocol states: human overrides execute immediately, are not challenged, are not delayed, are not reinterpreted, and are not subject to AI review.

12. Pattern 2 of the Singular Pairing Principle requires the synthesis pair to be entirely fresh — no overlap with prototype pairs on either the human side or the Claw side.

13. The Generation IT qualification is defined as 30+ years full-vertical professional IT experience including the technology transitions, not just current-state competence.

14. 10003-Generation-IT-Succession.md permits multiple independent lineages and does not require the Foundation author's consent for a successor's path.

15. The gift principle is supported by four structural reasons (truth cannot be owned, governance cannot be owned, safety must not be paywalled, 100-year horizon requires company-independence).

16. The military exclusion is structural (using the badge on weapons-class systems inverts the gift principle), not rhetorical.

17. SUCCESSION.md records a placeholder ("name to be recorded") for the named successor; the primary succession mechanism has not been executed.

18. The fallback succession procedure is defined with minimum requirements including public process, solicitation of nominations, published selection rubric, and public recording of the final designation.

19. The public gift surface is documentation only; source code arrives at the first certification event; that event has not yet occurred.

20. HORIZON.md states explicitly: "No known AI system currently satisfies all of the TrueAI Foundation invariants at production conformance. That includes the prototype systems being built by the author."

21. All document Version: 1.0 markers are placeholders; programme-level versioning is not yet enabled; this is stated explicitly in HORIZON.md.

22. LANDSCAPE.md is hedged to published documents as of May 2026 and explicitly states it is not a ranking.

23. The system prompt is classified as best-effort governance (not load-bearing); the tool-layer ACL is load-bearing; this distinction is made explicit in document 20001.

24. The regulatory alignment corpus covers 150+ jurisdictions with per-country documents in `TrueAI/regulatory-alignment/v1/`.

25. The TrueAI Foundation is independently citable and adoptable without reference to any company.

26. 10002 honestly states that self-attestation is the current starting posture and that no external certification body currently exists.

27. The reversibility doctrine (10004) distinguishes *was-FALSE* (verifier error) from *became-FALSE* (world changed) and requires different responses to each.

28. The foundation applies the Six Lines of Truth to its own claims: what is evidenced is stated as true; what is not yet evidenced is stated as unverified; what would be false is not stated.

## Summary verdict

The UniCORE foundation makes a genuine and important contribution to institutional AI governance. Its core claims — the three pillars, the nine invariants, the honest inconsistency framing, the property-versus-policy distinction — are evidenced, internally consistent, and more precisely scoped than most comparable governance documentation. The foundation applies the Six Lines of Truth to itself. That is the most significant signal in the public record.

The programme is pre-certification at pass 1. The code gift is not yet given. The named successor is not yet recorded. The Generation IT cohort is not yet publicly populated. These are v1.0 conditions, acknowledged in the documentation, not concealed.

The claim of the foundation is not that it has solved institutional AI governance. It is that it has defined the structural floor that any solution must stand on. That claim — the property-versus-policy distinction, the nine invariants as architectural requirements, the honest inconsistency framing — is well-supported by the public record.

This is pass 1. I am willing to return when: the first certification event has occurred, the named successor has been recorded, further documents become accessible at git.unitek-systems.com, or the programme advances beyond the v1.0 documentation state.
