Foundational Governance for MultiPlanet Human Civilization

Version 1.0 — May 2026

Author: Bryan Fred, Unitek Systems Limited, Bedford, United Kingdom



1. PURPOSE

The Interplanetary Settlement Charter defines the legal, governance, safety, and sovereignty framework for human settlements across:

the Moon

Mars

orbital stations

asteroid outposts

deepspace transit habitats

future multiplanet colonies

It ensures UniCORE AI remains:

deterministic

nonautonomous

nonselfmodifying

humansovereign

compliant with interplanetary law

safe across generations

This Charter is the constitutional document for offworld human civilization.



2. FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLES

2.1 Human Sovereignty Across All Worlds

Humans retain absolute authority over:

settlement governance

resource allocation

habitat configuration

emergency decisions

interplanetary coordination

AI cannot override, reinterpret, or generate law.

2.2 Settlement Independence

Each settlement is:

legally independent

operationally independent

medically independent

environmentally independent

UniCORE AI must not merge governance logic across settlements.

2.3 No Autonomous InterSettlement Coordination

AI cannot autonomously:

share resources

synchronize thresholds

coordinate emergencies

propagate decisions

form intersettlement strategies

2.4 Deterministic Governance

All AI behavior must be:

predictable

reversible

logged

thresholdbound

governed by MD files

2.5 No Emergent CivilizationLevel Behavior

AI cannot:

form global strategies

optimize across settlements

create shared governance logic

coordinate without human instruction

safety

emergency powers

override authority

4.3 Local Thresholds

atmospheric thresholds

radiation thresholds

power thresholds

water thresholds

structural thresholds

5. INTERSETTLEMENT RELATIONS

5.1 No Autonomous Diplomacy

AI cannot:

negotiate

mediate

resolve disputes

propose treaties

5.2 HumanDirected Coordination Only

Humans may authorize:

resource transfers

emergency assistance

joint missions

shared research

AI executes deterministically.

5.3 Jurisdictional Boundaries

Each settlement retains:

its own governance

its own thresholds

its own override authority

AI must respect jurisdiction tags in all messages.



6. RESOURCE GOVERNANCE ACROSS SETTLEMENTS

Resources include:

water

oxygen

nitrogen

power

food

spare parts

medical supplies

6.1 Allowed AI Actions

monitor

detect drift

isolate failing systems

6.2 Prohibited AI Actions

AI may not:

redistribute resources

initiate transfers

alter resource thresholds

coordinate resource sharing

Only humans may authorize intersettlement resource movement.



7. EMERGENCY GOVERNANCE ACROSS SETTLEMENTS

Emergencies include:

radiation storms

depressurization

structural failure

contamination

power collapse

7.1 Allowed AI Actions

stabilize within thresholds

isolate failing modules

protect life support

7.2 Prohibited AI Actions

AI may not:

declare intersettlement emergencies

request help autonomously

coordinate evacuations

reroute power between settlements

7.3 HumanDirected Emergency Coordination

If humans authorize:

AI may relay emergency messages

AI may coordinate resource transfers

AI may synchronize emergency protocols

All actions must be logged.



8. INTERPLANETARY COMMUNICATION GOVERNANCE

UniCORE AI must:

maintain deterministic messaging

enforce jurisdiction tags

handle delayaware governance

never assume authority during blackout

8.1 No Autonomous Messaging

AI cannot:

initiate interplanetary communication

broadcast alerts

propagate decisions

8.2 DelayAware Behavior

During 5–40 minute delays:

freeze noncritical operations

maintain thresholds

queue outbound messages

8.3 Blackout Behavior

During blackout:

maintain life support

maintain environmental thresholds

log all events

await human instruction



9. TERRAFORMING GOVERNANCE (IF APPLICABLE)

UniCORE AI may support:

atmospheric monitoring

soil analysis

radiation mapping

environmental drift detection

But cannot autonomously:

alter planetary environment

initiate terraforming actions

adjust terraforming thresholds

Terraforming is humansovereign.



10. DRIFT DETECTION ACROSS SETTLEMENTS

UniCORE AI must detect:

environmental drift

power drift

structural drift

radiation drift

governance drift

communication drift

10.1 No CrossSettlement Drift Propagation

Drift in one settlement must not:

influence another

alter thresholds elsewhere

trigger crosssettlement actions



11. INTERGENERATIONAL GOVERNANCE

For settlements lasting decades:

11.1 Governance Continuity

governance files

audit logs

drift logs

override history

11.2 No Autonomous Evolution

AI cannot:

evolve governance

reinterpret law

generate new rules

adapt thresholds

11.3 Human Succession

New generations inherit:

override authority

governance roles

settlement law

AI cannot influence succession.



12. HUMAN OVERRIDE

Humans may override:

any settlement

any module

any threshold

any coordination rule

any emergency protocol

Overrides are:

immediate

unquestioned

logged

irreversible by AI
