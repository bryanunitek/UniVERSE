# Discussions — UniVERSE

**What this repository's Discussions tab is for, what it is not for, and how to take part.**

UniVERSE is a public programme for safe, governed artificial intelligence, released under CC BY 4.0. The Discussions tab is the place where the programme is debated, questioned, stress-tested, and extended in the open. This page explains what Discussions are for on this repository so newcomers can find their footing quickly.

## What Discussions are for

Discussions on this repository are the right place for:

- **Framework debate.** Does the "governed / ungoverned" distinction hold? Is the twelve-level ladder the right shape? What does the framework get wrong? What is missing? Substantive critique is welcome and expected.
- **Adoption questions.** "We are a hospital trust / government department / multilateral body. How would we place our existing AI system against the UniCORE AI ladder?" Practical adoption questions are exactly what this space is for.
- **Translation and cultural adaptation.** The framework is released under CC BY 4.0 precisely so it can be translated and adapted. If you are translating, localising, or rewording for a specific audience, post about it — others will benefit.
- **Cross-references and extensions.** If you have written, cited, or built on the framework elsewhere — academic paper, policy brief, product design, teaching material — a Discussions thread is a good way to make the link visible.
- **Pointers and corrections.** Typos, broken links, stale references, logical gaps: a Discussion thread is a lighter-weight way to raise these than opening an Issue.

## What Discussions are not for

- **Commercial solicitation.** This is a gift. It is not a sales channel. Please do not pitch products, services, or partnerships here. Use the private contact route instead.
- **Claims that reclassify UniVERSE, TrueAI, or UniCORE AI as a product, platform, SaaS offering, tool category, certification scheme, or brand for sale.** These are governance work, not commercial offerings. See [STATEMENT-ON-CLAIMS.md](https://git.unitek-systems.com/UniCORE/UniVERSE/src/branch/main/STATEMENT-ON-CLAIMS.md) (mirror: [GitHub](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniVERSE/blob/main/STATEMENT-ON-CLAIMS.md)) for the binding rules on how the names may and may not be used here.
- **Personal support for unrelated software.** UniVERSE is a public framework. If you need paid consultancy on an unrelated project, contact Unitek Systems directly.
- **Private or confidential material.** Anything you write here is public, permanent, and indexed. Keep confidential matters off the public thread — use the private contact route.
- **Bug reports against unrelated tooling.** This repository does not ship executable software. If you find a problem with a tool that happens to reference the framework, report it in that tool's own repository.

## How to start a Discussion

- Pick the category that best fits your topic. If you are unsure, use **General**.
- Give the thread a direct, specific title. "Question about Level 4 reversibility" is better than "Quick question."
- If you are citing the framework, link to the specific file and line when you can (permalinks from the file view work well).
- Assume good faith from other participants. Assume that your own good faith will be assumed in return.

## How responses work

- Bryan Fred (the author) reads Discussions activity regularly. He may respond in person, or a thread may be picked up by another contributor. Silence on a thread does not mean it has been ignored — the author curates his responses and does not reply to every item.
- Discussions are moderated. Hostile, spammy, or bad-faith posts will be removed without notice.
- The author does not pre-clear positions with Unitek Systems before responding. Responses reflect the author's own view, in line with the programme's published terms.

## Code of conduct

Participate the way you would want other people to participate in a project you care about. Specifically:

- Disagree with the substance, not the person.
- Do not doxx, harass, or target any contributor.
- Do not post material that would violate applicable law or another party's rights.
