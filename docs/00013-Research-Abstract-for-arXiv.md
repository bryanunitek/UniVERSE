Title:

UniCORE AI: A TruthAnchored, NonSelfModifying Governance Architecture for LongDuration Artificial Intelligence Systems

Author:

Bryan Fred (Unitek Systems Limited)

Abstract:

We present UniCORE AI, a deterministic, truthanchored, multilevel governance architecture designed to ensure longduration safety, stability, and human sovereignty in advanced artificial intelligence systems. UniCORE AI is built on the TrueAI Foundation, a nonfabricating, nonselfmodifying framework that prohibits autonomous rule creation, emergent internal processes, and recursive selfimprovement. The architecture introduces a 12Level Governance Stack, where each level is humanauthored, humanbounded, and externally defined through immutable governance files. Truth flows upward through evidence and verification layers, while governance flows downward through humanauthored rule structures, culminating in Level 12: Human Governance, which remains sovereign and intentionally imperfect.

UniCORE AI enforces strict constraints including: (1) prohibition of AIgenerated architecture, (2) prohibition of autonomous heartbeats or internal lifecycles, (3) deterministic interlevel communication, (4) immutable governance MD files for legal, ethical, and missionspecific rules, and (5) the Reasonable Governance Threshold, which allows for human discretion and contextual judgement without enabling governance drift. The system is implemented using .NET 10, DevExpress XAF/XPO, and SQLbacked deterministic storage, with a functional prototype targeted for December 2026.

UniCORE AI is intended as a noncommercial, open governance foundation for enterprise systems, governmental frameworks, global compliance infrastructures, and longduration space missions, including human hibernation environments. This work proposes a centuryscale architecture for safe, governed artificial intelligence and invites collaboration from the global AI research community.
