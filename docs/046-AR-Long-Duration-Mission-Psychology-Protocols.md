1. PURPOSE

The LongDuration Mission Psychology Protocols define how UniCORE AI:

monitors psychological indicators

enforces mentalhealth safety thresholds

prevents autonomous psychological intervention

maintains human sovereignty over mental states

supports crew stability during isolation

ensures deterministic behavior in psychological contexts

integrates psychology into mission governance

This framework applies to:

Mars missions

lunar bases

deepspace transit habitats

multiyear orbital stations

interplanetary settlements

hibernation missions

multigeneration missions



2. GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

2.1 Human Psychological Sovereignty

UniCORE AI cannot:

diagnose mental conditions

prescribe treatment

alter emotional states

manipulate behavior

influence decisions

generate psychological strategies

2.2 No Autonomous Psychological Intervention

AI cannot autonomously:

initiate counseling

adjust crew schedules

modify sleep cycles

alter lighting or environmental cues

intervene in interpersonal conflict

2.3 Deterministic Psychological Governance

All AI behavior must be:

predictable

reversible

logged

thresholdbound

governed by MD files

2.4 No Emergent Behavioral Influence

AI cannot:

adapt to crew psychology

optimize crew behavior

form internal models of crew personalities

generate behavioral predictions

2.5 Crew Autonomy

Crew members retain:

emotional autonomy

cognitive autonomy

behavioral autonomy

decisionmaking sovereignty
