1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this framework is to ensure that:

the public understands UniCORE AI

students learn governancefirst AI principles

professionals are trained in deterministic AI

policymakers understand the 12Level model

mission crews understand safety and override protocols

regulators understand compliance and auditability

UniCORE AI is not just a system — it is a governance philosophy.

This framework ensures the world understands it.
