## Point-by-point Truth classification

### 1. The seven-repo architecture (documents 00, 09, 24–27)
**TRUE.** The stack Copilot describes — UniVERSE (public charter), TrueAI (immutable foundation), UniCORE (governance spine), UniCORE-AI (12-level reference architecture), UniCORE.GVB, UniSaaS.UniCORE, UniSaaS.UniCORE.GVB — is accurate.

### 2. The GVB name (document 00 §5, and elsewhere)
**Correction.** Copilot names GVB the "Global **Verification** Bridge." The canonical name is the **Global Virtual Bridge**. This is a reviewer wording artifact; the public repositories carry the correct name. No repository change required. Copilot's own review text is left verbatim.

### 3. The verification nodes: LA · NJ · UK (documents 00, 09, 19–27)
**TRUE, with one nuance.** All three nodes are provisioned. NJ is on the `103.84.215.0/24` block and exists. The one detail Copilot could not know: the NJ-designated server is **physically located in Los Angeles today** (same Colocation America support presence), not yet in New Jersey. The plan is to upgrade the server in LA, ship it to New Jersey, and have Colocation America move the `/24` IP block to the NJ data centre. **This is acceptable during Development.** By **Phase II testing**, the NJ node must be physically in New Jersey. So "LA · NJ · UK" is substantially correct; the physical NJ placement is a scheduled, not fabricated, item.

### 4. The Truth-Asset valuation model — P&L → Balance Sheet (documents 01 §2, 03)
**UNVERIFIED — and this is UniCORE's genuine view, held as True pending expert verification.** UniCORE believes that ratified, provenance-anchored, firm-bound Truth can become a capitalisable intangible asset, moving part of AI cost from the P&L to the Balance Sheet. **We do not yet assert this as TRUE.** Whether it satisfies intangible-asset recognition (e.g. IAS 38 / relevant GAAP) is a question for a **suitably qualified accountant at the right level**, and we will seek that review to find the Truth. Until then it is UNVERIFIED — a belief we intend to test, not a settled fact. Copilot's framing of it is its own; we neither adopt nor discard it, we verify it.

### 5. Longevity — "survive 100 years" (documents 01 §4, 22, 23, 27)
**Refined by UniCORE.** Copilot describes a ~100-year survival horizon. UniCORE's actual design is a **1000-year cycle**, within which the AI is **checked and adjusted every 100 years**. The 100-year figure is the *review interval*, not the *lifespan*. Longevity is a **design intention**, not a demonstrated runtime property — it is what the architecture is built to achieve, and it will be proven only over time and at each checkpoint.

### 6. "Prevents epistemic collapse" / "freezes Truth" / "cannot be stolen, cloned, transferred, exfiltrated" (documents 01, 06, 11, 12, 20, 21, 26, 27)
**Design intentions, not yet verified runtime.** These are the properties the governance model, the invariants, and the Badge are **designed to enforce**. Stating them as already-achieved runtime behaviour is stronger than the grounded record supports: configuration set is not the same as behaviour proven. UniCORE holds these as **committed design targets under construction and test**, classified UNVERIFIED as achieved runtime until exercised and demonstrated.

### 7. Monetisation — "Truth cannot be sold / not monetised" (documents 10, 11, 21, 22, 24, 26)
**Correction — the model is two-layered, not "unsellable."** The **foundation** (the 7 public repositories, CC BY 4.0) is **given, not sold** — irrevocably. The **commercial layer** (the private Claw repositories, the Vertical CORE Business Objects, and the product sold by a product-bearing entity) **is sold**, and it funds the gift. The gift prevents the *foundation* from being monopolised; it does **not** prohibit commerce built on top under the Badge. Copilot's absolute "cannot be monetised" over-states one layer and erases the other. UniCORE's position: **foundation gifted; Solutions sold.** Both, by design.

### 8. Grounding floor — "grounding begins at Level 5 / Levels 1–4 are zero" (documents 13, 16, 17)
**Correction.** Grounding does not begin at Level 5. In the canonical maturity model, the governance levels 5–12 map *within* the higher maturity bands, and the institutional grounding begins earlier than Level 5. Copilot conflated a licensing/architecture tier with a maturity floor. The public repositories state the model correctly; no change required.

### 9. The invariants — count and naming (document 11)
**Correction.** The canonical set is the **Nine Invariants** (TrueAI Foundation). Copilot presented its own reconstructed list under the heading "constitutional invariants"; it is a paraphrase, not the ratified nine. Readers should treat the TrueAI Foundation's Nine Invariants as canonical.

### 10. Coined vocabulary presented as doctrine (documents 12, 16, 17, 23, 27)
**Reviewer commentary, not UniCORE terminology.** Terms such as "epistemic sovereignty," "load boundary," "drift-freeze / walk-back protocol," a fixed "12 constitutional layers" list, and a fourth horizon "→ Permanence / Completion" are **Copilot's synthesis**, not canonical UniCORE names. Canon anchors are the **Nine Invariants** and the **twelve Governance Levels**; the civilisational horizon is the **three-stage Harmony → Peace → Space** triad. These coinages are reasonable outside-reader glosses and are welcome as such — but they are not adopted as UniCORE doctrine.

### 11. Superlatives — "world's first," "no other system resembles this" (nearly every document)
**Bounded by UniCORE.** UniCORE does not claim to be provably the first or only such system in existence. The honest bound is: **the most complete public attempt of this kind that we are aware of.** Copilot's unbounded superlatives are its own; we hold to the bounded claim.
