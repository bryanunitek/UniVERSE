# Claw

**Programme vocabulary: governed channels, independent connections, and the modality-agnostic layer**

Author: Bryan Fred, Unitek Systems Limited, Bedford, United Kingdom
First published: May 2026
Status: Public. Given, not sold. Irrevocable.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the word **Claw** as it is used across the
UniVERSE programme. It names the difference between a governed
human-AI channel and an independent personal AI connection. It states
the architectural commitment that governance is modality-agnostic —
that the Claw concept extends to whatever connectivity exists today
or emerges tomorrow.

The word *Claw* is already in active use across programme materials.
[TrueAI 10001](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI/blob/main/docs/10001-Singular-Pairing-Principle.md)
binds production work to "one human, one AI Claw per workstream."
[00057 Layered CORE Model](00057-Layered-CORE-Model.md) names the
`-Claw` suffix as the marker for L3 Vertical COREs in the Law sector.
This document pins the vocabulary down so the same word is read the
same way in every programme document, every README, every
regulatory submission, and every future code reference.

## 2. The terms

The Claw vocabulary has four structural terms (Claw, ExternalClaw,
PairedClaw, UniCORE.Law-Claw) and one term that is a product brand
rather than a structural noun (MyClaw, see [§2.6](#26-myclaw)).

This revision replaces an earlier three-term reading of Claw /
UniCORE.Law-Claw / MyClaw, in which MyClaw was described as an
ungoverned personal AI connection sitting outside the Claw family
entirely. The corrected reading places MyClaw inside the Claw family
as a brand of ExternalClaw, names PairedClaw as the structural bond
between an ExternalClaw and a UniCORE Solution, and reserves
AgentClaw as a sibling brand of ExternalClaw for autonomous
AI-driven Claws without a human owner. The architectural commitments
of the document (modality-agnostic governance, substrate-agnostic
governance, the Singular Pairing relationship) are unchanged; only
the vocabulary is sharper. Per
[HORIZON.md Versioning is not yet enabled](../HORIZON.md#versioning-is-not-yet-enabled),
revisions to programme material are recorded in git history rather
than in the `Version` line; the `Version: 1.0` placeholder on every
programme document is read accordingly.

### 2.1 Claw

A **Claw** is a *governed* human-AI interaction channel. It is the
channel, not the AI behind it.

A Claw carries the governance of the layer it serves. A Claw
operating under the TrueAI Foundation carries the Nine Invariants
into the interaction. A Claw operating under UniCORE AI carries the
inter-level messaging protocol, the Reasonable Governance Threshold,
and the Human Override Protocol. A Claw operating under an L3
Scope-Domain CORE carries the additional governance of that CORE
into the interaction as well.

The AI provider behind a Claw is interchangeable. OpenAI today,
Anthropic today, a local model today, a future provider tomorrow.
The provider is a substrate. The Claw is the governed interface
that sits between a human and that substrate. What makes a Claw a
Claw is the governance it carries, not the model it runs on.

A single Claw operates with one human and one workstream
(see [TrueAI 10001](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI/blob/main/docs/10001-Singular-Pairing-Principle.md)).
This is a governance requirement of the Foundation, not a property
of the Claw concept itself. The Singular Pairing Principle binds
production work to 1 human + 1 Claw; the Claw concept does not
forbid other shapes outside Foundation-aligned work.

### 2.2 UniCORE.Law-Claw

**UniCORE.Law-Claw** is the first L3 Scope-Domain CORE — the Vertical
CORE for the Law sector, produced by Unitek. It is also, by direct
implication, the first reference implementation of the Claw concept
itself.

Two readings of the name UniCORE.Law-Claw are both correct and
intentional:

1. *A reference library that produces governed Law-sector Claws.*
   The CORE supplies the Business Objects, Business Logic, schema,
   and configuration that a Law Firm Solution uses to operate its
   Claws against the Foundation.
2. *The first proof that the Claw model can be implemented.* Other
   verticals (Medical, Accountancy, etc.) and other scope domains
   (jurisdictions, bodies, network fabrics — see
   [00057 §2](00057-Layered-CORE-Model.md#2-the-four-tiers)) will produce their own
   COREs and their own Claws. UniCORE.Law-Claw demonstrates the
   pattern.

The naming convention `UniCORE-<vertical>-Claw` for L3 Vertical COREs
(see [00057 §5](00057-Layered-CORE-Model.md#5-naming-conventions))
takes its `-Claw` suffix from this same vocabulary. The CORE is
named for the Claw it produces, with the vertical token naming the
scope domain it serves.

### 2.3 ExternalClaw

An **ExternalClaw** is any Claw that does not live inside a UniCORE
Enterprise Solution. It is governed by the Foundation directly
rather than by a Solution that contains it. The structural property
is location relative to a Solution, not absence of governance.

ExternalClaws may connect into a UniCORE Solution. When such a
connection is established under OpenClaw Connect, the resulting
bond is itself a structural noun named PairedClaw
(see [§2.4](#24-pairedclaw)). The ExternalClaw remains external; the
bond is what brings it into a Solution's audit and policy envelope
for the duration of the connection.

ExternalClaw is the parent noun for at least two concrete brands:

- **MyClaw** — a person's own Claw. Human-owned. See
  [§2.6](#26-myclaw) for the product distinction.
- **AgentClaw** — an autonomous AI-driven Claw with no human
  owner. Its independent action is realised by an AI brain.
  The list of supported AI providers for the brain inside an
  AgentClaw (and for any other Claw that carries an AI brain) is
  maintained in
  [00060 Supported AI Provider List](00060-Supported-AI-Provider-List.md).

Further brands of ExternalClaw may emerge as the programme matures
(for example, observer-style Claws that witness cascade events
without acting, or oracle-style Claws that answer Foundation-state
queries without originating action). The vocabulary reserves the
ExternalClaw slot for future siblings; today MyClaw and AgentClaw
are the named brands.

### 2.4 PairedClaw

A **PairedClaw** is the structural bond between an ExternalClaw and
a UniCORE.Law-Claw (or any other `UniCORE-<vertical>-Claw`) Solution.
It is a noun: the bond itself is an audited row in the Solution,
with a lifecycle, an identity, and a governance posture distinct
from either side of the bond.

A PairedClaw exists for the duration of a connection from an
ExternalClaw into a Solution under OpenClaw Connect. Inside the
Solution, the PairedClaw carries the principal identity, the scope,
the authority class, and the audit trail of the bonded ExternalClaw
for as long as the bond holds. When the connection ends or is
revoked, the PairedClaw row remains for audit purposes; a fresh row
is required to re-establish a bond.

A PairedClaw is what the
[Singular Pairing Principle](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI/blob/main/docs/10001-Singular-Pairing-Principle.md)
actually binds. One human, one Claw per workstream means one human,
one active PairedClaw per workstream. The ExternalClaw on the other
side of the bond may be a MyClaw or an AgentClaw; the Singular
Pairing rule applies to the bond, not to either endpoint in
isolation.

This document names PairedClaw at the vocabulary level. The schema,
lifecycle states, and connection protocol live in the relevant
L3 Vertical CORE repository
([UniCORE.Law-Claw](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE.Law-Claw)
for the Law sector) and in the OpenClaw Connect protocol
documentation.

#### Bond admission rule

A PairedClaw bond is admitted only when the bonding ExternalClaw
carries a valid UNICOREMASTER-authored bond file at its declared
Level, and the Solution's session-opening protocol verifies the bond
from `UNVERIFIED` to `TRUE`. The bond file is the credential; the
session-opening protocol is the runtime verification.

The bond carries explicit version pins for the Foundation
requirements, the per-Level corpus, and the programme corpus that the
Solution is operating against. Version drift between the Solution's
pins and the canonical heads of the public repositories is surfaced
to UNICOREMASTER per the failure ladder. The Solution does not
silently track corpus evolution; it operates at the versions it was
built against until its operators redeploy.

The full bond mechanism, including the session-opening protocol, the
badge (`PairedClaw UniCORE AI Secured`), the `UNVERIFIED → TRUE`
lifecycle, and the agent's right to return the bond to `UNVERIFIED`
mid-session, is specified in [00061 PairedClaw Bond File And Session
Protocol](00061-PairedClaw-Bond-File-And-Session-Protocol.md). The
failure ladder, PAUSE state, and EMERGENCY mechanism are specified in
[00062 Pairing Failure Ladder, PAUSE Mode And EMERGENCY](00062-Pairing-Failure-Ladder-Pause-Mode-And-EMERGENCY.md).

### 2.5 The four structural terms together

```
Claw                          (abstract: any governed channel under Foundation governance)
├─ ExternalClaw               (Claw that lives OUTSIDE a UniCORE Solution)
│  ├─ MyClaw                  (brand: person's own Claw, human-owned)
│  └─ AgentClaw               (brand: autonomous AI-driven Claw, no human owner)
│
└─ PairedClaw                 (the BOND: ExternalClaw <-> UniCORE.Law-Claw,
                                 established via OpenClaw Connect)

UniCORE.Law-Claw              (the deployed vertical Solution that HOSTS PairedClaws;
                                 distinct from the Claws on either side of any bond)
```

The taxonomy is shape-only. It does not name how many Claws of each
brand exist, which AI brain powers any given Claw, or what
workstream a PairedClaw is bound into. Those properties belong to
the specific Solution and the specific bond, not to the vocabulary.

### 2.6 MyClaw (the product) and MyClaw (the structural noun)

The word **MyClaw** is also a product brand. [myclaw.ai](https://myclaw.ai?via=bryan)
is a managed OpenClaw hosting platform, distinct from this
programme. A MyClaw account at myclaw.ai is one concrete way to
run a MyClaw-brand ExternalClaw — the product name and the
structural noun deliberately coincide.

A person who runs their own OpenClaw on their own infrastructure
is equally running a MyClaw in the structural sense. The product
brand and the self-hosted case are read identically at the
structural level. The programme's vocabulary is about the
structural property (a person's own Claw, owned by them, external
to any UniCORE Solution), not about the hosting choice that
realises it.

Where this document refers to a MyClaw without qualification, the
structural reading applies. Where a specific hosting provider is
relevant, the document names it explicitly.

## 3. Why four structural terms

The distinction between the four structural terms is structural,
not cosmetic. Each term names a position that matters for
governance, and naming only three of them would leave a gap.

A **Claw** is the abstract: any governed human-AI or AI-AI channel
under Foundation governance.

An **ExternalClaw** is a Claw that lives outside a Solution. It is
governed by the Foundation directly. A MyClaw at work on a personal
research task is an ExternalClaw. An AgentClaw running a long-horizon
autonomous workstream is an ExternalClaw. The brand differs; the
structural position does not.

A **PairedClaw** is the bond that brings an ExternalClaw into a
Solution's governance envelope for the duration of a connection.
Output produced through an active PairedClaw can be claimed as
Foundation-aligned, can carry the attribution couplet
(see [00057 §4](00057-Layered-CORE-Model.md#4-attribution)), and is
auditable against the Solution's L3 CORE governance, the L2 UniCORE
AI governance, and the L1 Foundation governance.

A **UniCORE.Law-Claw** (or any other `UniCORE-<vertical>-Claw`) is
the Solution that hosts PairedClaws. It is not a brand of Claw on
either side of the bond. It is the envelope inside which bonds
live.

Output from an ExternalClaw operating without a PairedClaw is
Foundation-governed but not Solution-governed. It cannot carry a
Solution's attribution couplet. It can carry the Foundation's
certification mark when Foundation governance was the operative
layer. Mixing output from a Foundation-only ExternalClaw into
Solution-aligned work without explicit provenance is a governance
failure on the human side, not a property of either tool.

The programme names all four terms because all four positions exist.
A vocabulary that named only the Solution side, or only the
external side, or that conflated the bond with either endpoint,
would leave a structural gap that cannot be defended.

## 4. Modality-agnostic governance

A Claw is not bound to any particular communication shape.

Today, a Claw is most often a chat interface or an API session. As
connectivity evolves — agentic protocols, voice-first interfaces,
embodied interaction, inter-process AI-to-AI channels under human
authority, brain-computer interfaces in some future, whatever new
modalities emerge — the Claw concept extends to cover each new
shape.

UniCORE AI (L2) is the **modality-agnostic governance layer**. Its
job is to make Foundation governance flow through whatever channel
shape the human and the AI are using. The modality changes; the
governance does not.

This is the architectural commitment that protects the programme
from being made obsolete by a change in interaction technology. A
Claw built today, governed under TrueAI through UniCORE AI, can be
extended to a new modality tomorrow without renegotiating its
governance posture. The same Foundation, the same nine invariants,
the same audit trail.

A MyClaw, by definition, does not participate in this commitment.
A future-modality MyClaw is still a MyClaw — outside programme
governance, regardless of how advanced its interaction shape becomes.

### 4.1 Substrate-agnostic governance

The modality-agnostic property has a sister property at a lower
layer of the stack: governance is also **substrate-agnostic**.

The model that drives a Claw at any given moment is one substrate
choice among many. Over a Solution's hundred-year or thousand-year
horizon, the model substrate will be replaced many times. The
Foundation, the audit trail, the corpus, and the principal identity
surface remain. The substrate is replaceable; the governance
layer is not.

This matters for two recurring questions about a governed AI
deployment.

**Why a future model substitution does not weaken governance.**
The governance layer never lived inside the model in the first
place. The boundary surfaces — input validation, tool-layer ACLs,
the Reasonable Governance Threshold, the Human Override Protocol,
attribution, audit, identity, certification mark — are unchanged
by a model swap. A new model reads the same governed substrate the
old one did. Full treatment in
[UniCORE-AI 20001 — Why the Rules Don't Live in the Prompt](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE-AI/blob/main/docs/20001-Why-Rules-Do-Not-Live-In-The-Prompt.md).

**Why a saturating context window does not weaken governance.**
The model's context window is working memory; the governed substrate
is the system of record. When the window saturates, a fixed
compaction rule keeps a documented pinned set in active memory and
summarises the rest with hash-anchored references to the durable
record. Compaction is itself a recorded governed event. Full
treatment in
[UniCORE-AI 20002 — Compaction and Durable Continuity](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE-AI/blob/main/docs/20002-Compaction-And-Durable-Continuity.md).

The two properties — modality-agnostic and substrate-agnostic — are
the pair of architectural commitments that let a Solution outlast
any particular interaction shape and any particular model.

## 5. Reading the vocabulary across programme documents

Where existing documents say "AI Claw" or "Claw" without
qualification, the meaning is the generic Claw of [§2.1](#21-claw) — a
governed channel.

Where existing documents say "UniCORE-Claw" without further
qualification, the meaning is the specific L3 Vertical CORE for the
Law sector — the same CORE this document now refers to as
UniCORE.Law-Claw under the `UniCORE-<vertical>-Claw` naming
convention adopted in
[00057 §5](00057-Layered-CORE-Model.md#5-naming-conventions).
"UniCORE-Claw" is the prior name; "UniCORE.Law-Claw" is the current
canonical name. The repository, the codebase, and the Vertical CORE
itself are unchanged; only the public name has been clarified to
make the vertical token explicit.

Going forward, the name `UniCORE-Claw` is **reserved** for a separate,
future artefact: a vertical-neutral L3 CORE *template* from which any
vertical-specific L3 CORE may be derived. That artefact does not yet
exist as a published repository. When it is created, it will be
distinct from UniCORE.Law-Claw and from any other
`UniCORE-<vertical>-Claw`. Until then, any reference to "UniCORE-Claw"
in programme materials older than 2026-05-15 should be read as a prior
name for UniCORE.Law-Claw.

This is consistent with [§2.2](#22-unicore-law-claw).

Where programme materials predating this revision refer to MyClaw
as ungoverned or as not-a-Claw, the corrected reading is the
four-term taxonomy of [§2](#2-the-terms): MyClaw is a brand of
ExternalClaw, governed by the Foundation directly, capable of
entering Solution governance through a PairedClaw bond when the
user brings it into a Solution context via OpenClaw Connect.

Where existing documents say "paired Claw" in lowercase narrative
prose, the meaning is the structural noun PairedClaw of
[§2.4](#24-pairedclaw) regardless of capitalisation in the source.

This revision is recorded in the git history of the UniVERSE
repository; per
[HORIZON.md](../HORIZON.md#versioning-is-not-yet-enabled), the
`Version: 1.0` placeholder is unchanged. Revision history below in
[§8](#8-status) is the narrative record of structural changes to
this document until programme-level versioning turns on.

## 6. Relationship to other programme commitments

- The **Singular Pairing Principle**
  ([TrueAI 10001](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI/blob/main/docs/10001-Singular-Pairing-Principle.md))
  binds production work to one human + one Claw. This document
  defines the word *Claw* it relies on, and names the bond
  (PairedClaw, [§2.4](#24-pairedclaw)) that Singular Pairing actually
  binds.
- **OpenClaw Connect** is the protocol by which an ExternalClaw
  establishes a PairedClaw bond into a Solution. The protocol
  itself is documented in the OpenClaw repository; this document
  names the vocabulary the protocol's lifecycle states refer to.
- The **list of supported AI providers** for any Claw that carries
  an AI brain is maintained in
  [00060 Supported AI Provider List](00060-Supported-AI-Provider-List.md).
  The list applies to MyClaws, AgentClaws, and to Claws inside any
  `UniCORE-<vertical>-Claw` Solution uniformly. The provider is the
  substrate; the Claw is the governed interface
  ([§4.1](#41-substrate-agnostic-governance)).
- The **Layered CORE Model**
  ([00057](00057-Layered-CORE-Model.md))
  names UniCORE.Law-Claw as the first L3 Vertical CORE and uses the
  `UniCORE-<vertical>-Claw` pattern as the naming convention for L3
  Vertical COREs. This document defines the underlying word the
  pattern points at.
- **Generation IT Succession**
  ([TrueAI 10003](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI/blob/main/docs/10003-Generation-IT-Succession.md))
  binds the human side of the Claw pairing to a qualification
  standard. This document is independent of who the human is; it
  defines the channel the qualified human uses.

## 7. What this document is not

- It is not a list of approved Claws. There is no programme-wide
  registry of governed channels. Each Solution and each CORE is
  responsible for its own Claws and the governance they carry.
- It is not a list of forbidden MyClaws. The programme does not
  forbid personal AI use; it names it as outside programme
  governance and does not certify it.
- It is not a technical specification of how a Claw is implemented.
  Implementation lives in the relevant CORE repositories. This
  document is vocabulary.
- It is not a claim that "Claw" is a generic English word. The
  programme uses the word in this specific sense, distinct from
  any unrelated meaning the word may have in other contexts.

This document, like all programme documents, is evolving. The four
structural terms — Claw, ExternalClaw, PairedClaw, UniCORE.Law-Claw
— are settled at this revision. MyClaw and AgentClaw are named
brands of ExternalClaw and are also settled. Further
brands of ExternalClaw may be added as the programme matures; the
structural slot is reserved for them in [§2.3](#23-externalclaw).

programme-level versioning is not yet enabled, per
[HORIZON.md](../HORIZON.md#versioning-is-not-yet-enabled)):

  Claw, UniCORE.Law-Claw, MyClaw. MyClaw was described as ungoverned
  and as not-a-Claw. This reading is superseded by the four-term
  taxonomy of [§2](#2-the-terms); see
  [§2.6](#26-myclaw) and

- **Current revision (May 2026).** Four structural terms (Claw,
  ExternalClaw, PairedClaw, UniCORE.Law-Claw) plus named brands of
  ExternalClaw (MyClaw, AgentClaw). PairedClaw added as the
