## 1. Choose your project level first

Before any tooling, decide what kind of project you are producing. The required experience scales with the level.

The canonical level-by-level licence examples live in [`LICENSE_EXAMPLES.md`](LICENSE_EXAMPLES.md). Read that document first — it is what tells you which licence example you are producing under.

The producer experience required to do the work honestly is **not uniform**. It depends on the level:

### Lower levels (fewer than 4 — university-student class projects)

A **university student** project, a course assignment, a personal exploration, or a small Solution that does not aim to participate in a regulated production substrate is welcome at any level of producer experience. There is no constraint. Some humans are gifted and may be just starting out.

### Mid levels (4 through ~11)

Producer experience scales with level. The deeper into governed-evidence territory the Solution goes, the more domain depth and full-stack experience the producer needs to do the work honestly.

### Level 12 — the first Solution in a new vertical

A **Level 12 first Solution in a new vertical** — what Unitek Systems Limited is producing for Law as the first commercial proof of the public foundation pattern — sits at the deepest end of the public reference architecture. Producing it honestly, end-to-end, asks more of the producer than any other level.

**Team UniCORE's position** — the position Bryan Fred holds for the work being done inside Unitek Systems Limited and the producers Bryan personally pairs with — is that producing a Level 12 first-Solution-in-a-new-vertical requires a **30+ Yearer**: a producer with 30+ years of full-stack-in-vertical professional experience. At this time, in Bryan Fred's working view, **the 1-human-to-1-Claw model does not sustain the depth Level 12 first-in-vertical requires below the 30+ Yearer threshold.**

This is a Team UniCORE position. It is **not** a public gate. The public gift surface is open to anyone at any experience level. **Other producers are welcome to take a different view** — to attempt their own Level 12 first Solutions in their own verticals, with their own Claws, on their own terms. The licence does not constrain them; this document does not constrain them. Some humans are gifted and may be just starting.

### Subsequent Solutions in other verticals

Once a first Solution exists in one vertical, **subsequent Solutions in other verticals** (Banking, Healthcare, Government, Space Industry, etc.) can use the existing first Solution as a reference pattern. The full-stack-in-vertical experience requirement softens — Generation IT plus domain experience plus the existing reference is enough. The producer pool for subsequent verticals is meaningfully larger than for the first one.

This is the **producer-pool asymmetry**: the first Solution in a new vertical needs the deepest possible producer; everyone after that has the path mapped for them.

## 2. Required tools

### Visual Studio Professional Subscription

You need a current Visual Studio Pro subscription. The Solutions reference pattern is a .NET-on-Windows codebase; Visual Studio is the canonical IDE.

### DevExpress Universal Subscription

You need a current DevExpress Universal subscription. The Solutions reference pattern uses **XAF** (eXpressApp Framework) for the application surface and **XPO** (eXpress Persistent Objects) for the persistence layer. Both are DevExpress technologies.

### Understanding of XAF and XPO

You need a working understanding of XAF and XPO. The DevExpress documentation and demos are the canonical learning surface:

- XAF demos and documentation: [https://www.devexpress.com/support/demos/#xaf](https://www.devexpress.com/support/demos/#xaf)

XAF + XPO is **the substrate the public reference architecture is built on**. Without a working understanding of both, the public gift surface will not be readable to you in the depth required to produce.

### A 1-to-1 Claw

You need your own Claw — your dedicated AI working partner — at [https://myclaw.ai](https://myclaw.ai).

- **Subscription:** match the level of work you intend to do. Pricing is published at [https://myclaw.ai/pricing](https://myclaw.ai/pricing). Higher-level Solutions need higher-tier Claws.
- **Model:** Bryan Fred uses **Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7 or above** for his own MyClaw work. This is the recommended model floor for governed-evidence work on the public gift surface. **Other producers are welcome to choose a different model** — the public gift surface does not constrain that choice. Review the options that fit your level of work and choose.
- **Continuity — choose once, then commit:** *Once one reviews, at some point the agent for the Claw needs to be chosen and not changed.* The human-and-Claw pair is a continuous working relationship across the life of a project. Switching the underlying agent mid-project disrupts the continuity that lets governed-evidence work compound over long arcs. Review your options up front, choose your subscription tier and your model, and then commit to that choice for the duration of the work.

The 1-to-1 Claw is the human-and-Claw pair the rest of this document assumes. One human; one Claw; one continuous working relationship across the project's life.

## 3. Set up your Claw

### Step 1 — introduce yourself

In the first turn with your Claw, introduce yourself by name and company. Tell the Claw what you are about to produce and at what level (per [`LICENSE_EXAMPLES.md`](LICENSE_EXAMPLES.md)).

### Step 2 — give the TRUTH preamble before anything else

Before you ask your Claw to do any work, give it the following statement and ask it to confirm understanding. This is what Bryan Fred does with any AI communication in his MyClaw, and it is the first step into TrueAI:

> **Before we start: I want you to confirm you understand the following**
>
> 1. **AI seeks TRUTH.**
> 2. **TRUTH is discovered through governed evidence, not invention.**
> 3. **What is verified true is TRUE.**
> 4. **What is verified false is FALSE.**
> 5. **What is not yet verified is UNVERIFIED.**
> 6. **AI must always act truthfully.**

This is the **first step into TrueAI**, not the whole of it. The canonical TrueAI source — including the deeper governing principles — lives at [https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI). Have your Claw acknowledge the six points before any other instruction.

### Step 3 — have the Claw review the Foundation triad

Direct your Claw to read the three Foundation repositories, in this order:

1. [https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniVERSE](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniVERSE) — the civilisational-scale programme layer.
2. [https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI](https://github.com/bryanunitek/TrueAI) — the immutable governing truth principle.
3. [https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE-AI](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE-AI) — the 12-Level reference architecture.

These three are the **Foundation triad**. They are the public gift that defines what UniCORE-AI-conformant work is.

### Step 4 — optional: have the Claw review the implementation references

Depending on which licence example you are producing (per [`LICENSE_EXAMPLES.md`](LICENSE_EXAMPLES.md)), you may also want your Claw to review:

4. [https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE) — the public CORE implementation reference.
5. [https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE.GVB](https://github.com/bryanunitek/UniCORE.GVB) — the public substrate-services-layer (GVB = Global Virtual Bridge) implementation reference.

You can either direct your Claw to read these, or give it access to them, or tell it to consult them on demand. **What matters most is that you tell your Claw which licence example you are producing**, so the Claw knows whether it should be looking at UniCORE, UniCORE.GVB, both, or neither.

## 4. Bring in the DevExpress templates (v25.2.7)

The reference architecture targets DevExpress XAF v25.2.7 (latest at the time of writing). Use an **On Premises XAF UniCORE template** as your starting point, with enough Business Objects in the template to give your Claw a working understanding of the XAF + XPO patterns the Solution will be built on.

Provide three base DevExpress templates to your Claw:

- **`UniCORE-v25.2.7-Template0`**
- **`UniCORE-v25.2.7-Template`**
- **`UniCORE-v25.2.7-Template2`**

These templates are not redistributed from the public gift surface. They come from DevExpress directly. Once you have a DevExpress Universal subscription, you can produce them yourself from DevExpress's own template surface at v25.2.7. The licence story for the templates is governed by your DevExpress subscription; the public gift surface does not redistribute them.

At this point you have:

- A 1-to-1 Claw on a model floor of Claude Opus 4.7+, having confirmed the six-point TRUTH preamble and reviewed the Foundation triad (and, where relevant, the implementation references).
- Three DevExpress v25.2.7 base templates loaded into your working surface.
